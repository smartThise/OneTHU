# Getting started


[中文](../getting-started.md) · English

Download the installer for macOS, Windows, or Android from the [download page](https://onethu.github.io/en/download.html), which offers Tsinghua Cloud direct links, GitHub mirrors, version details, and SHA256 checksums. Installers are also published on the [release page](https://github.com/smartThise/OneTHU/releases/latest).

Use the **语言 / Language** toggle above the sign-in form—or on the two-factor verification screens—to switch between Chinese and English at any point. Changing the display language preserves what you have already typed, keeps your active sign-in flow going, and saves your preference across restarts. App messages translate immediately, while upstream notices from university servers appear in their original text.

First launch opens with a bilingual privacy and permissions overview. Cloud sync and OH agent services are opt-in and off by default, and selecting Full mode prompts for cloud-sync consent. Next, pick your guide language and choose Minimal, Practical, or Full mode. Every walkthrough covers search, OH, and plugin basics, while Full mode adds interactive tours of whiteboards, staging boxes, short codes, NFC, organizations, and Xiaoda—opening real app views populated with fictional sample data and providing a quick button to return to the guide.

Sign in with your Tsinghua account and complete second-factor verification when prompted, staying in the same verification session so your challenge code remains valid. Mail, calendars, cloud storage, and AI model providers connect with their own separate credentials when you enable them. If your university account has reached its maximum number of trusted devices, remove an old entry on the official Tsinghua account-management portal before registering a new one.

The OH capsule gives you quick search across all three modes, and OneShot starts with Study, Reservations, Life, Information, and Schedule. Full mode unlocks tabs, OneGO, customizable whiteboards, and extensions, while **My** surfaces the settings that match your current mode; see [Compare modes](experience-modes.md).

Use the in-page refresh button to reload a service's latest data, or the floating refresh control to reset broader view state. If your session expires or prompts for sign-in, follow the login prompt to re-authenticate cleanly. When reporting an issue, note your app version, OS, active experience mode, and redacted logs—see [troubleshooting](privacy-and-troubleshooting.md).
